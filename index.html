Church Hub Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Church Hub handles information when you use the platform.

1. Information We Collect

Church Hub may collect the following information when you create and use an account:

  • Email address — used for account authentication, sign-in and password recovery.
  • Full name — used to identify your profile within Church Hub.
  • Church affiliation — used to connect your account with a church on the platform.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Church Hub does not request or intentionally collect:

  • Precise location data.
  • Phone numbers unless a future feature specifically requires them and you are informed.
  • Payment or financial information directly through the app.
  • Unnecessary device identifiers.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided to Church Hub is used to:

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Maintain your user profile.
  • Connect users with registered churches.
  • Provide church discovery and content features.
  • Provide notifications and other requested app functionality.

4. Church Content

Registered churches and authorized Church Hub administrators may publish church-related information and content, including announcements, sermons, videos, worship audio, events, pastor notes and other church information.

Church content is intended to be publicly available within the Church Hub platform where the relevant church or platform administrator has published it.

5. Data Storage and Security

Church Hub uses Supabase for authentication, database and storage services. Information is stored using the services configured for the Church Hub platform.

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Church Hub uses third-party services, including Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database and storage.

Church Hub also uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on applicable law, your consent choices and your device settings, advertising services may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information and information about interactions with advertisements in order to provide, measure or personalize advertising.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review the applicable privacy policies of those services.

7. Account and Data Controls

You may access and update information associated with your Church Hub profile through the available profile and settings features.

You may also log out of your account or request deletion of your account through the available account controls.

8. Account Deletion

Church Hub provides an account deletion option within the user account area.

When an account deletion request is submitted, Church Hub will process the deletion of applicable user data according to the platform’s account-deletion process and applicable legal requirements.

9. Children

Church Hub is not specifically directed at children. Users should only create an account when they are legally permitted to do so in their jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Church Hub adds new features, changes its services or when applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be made available within the Church Hub application.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, account-data questions or requests concerning your personal information, please contact the Church Hub platform administrator.
